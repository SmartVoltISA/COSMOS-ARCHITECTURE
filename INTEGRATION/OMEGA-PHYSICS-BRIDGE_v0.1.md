## 9. What this gives COSMOS

The recovered Ω material contributes four reusable research primitives:

### A. Relational state

Treat the state of a physical system as more than a list of scalar observables; preserve relations between observables and between structural components.

### B. Distinguishability

When testing collapse, compactification, or regime transitions, explicitly measure whether distinct states remain distinguishable.

### C. Density / concentration

Density and concentration can be tested as candidate regime variables rather than assumed to be explanatory by themselves.

### D. Irreversibility / recoverability

If a COSMOS hypothesis invokes information loss, it must define an observable or operational criterion for loss of recoverable state information.
