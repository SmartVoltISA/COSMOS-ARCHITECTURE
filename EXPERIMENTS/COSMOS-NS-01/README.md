# COSMOS-NS-01 — Compact-Core Search

## Objective

Screen astronomical catalogues for objects whose measured properties are inconsistent with conventional planetary or stellar structure models and evaluate compact-core explanations.

## First observables

- mass M and uncertainty
- radius R and uncertainty
- mean density
- compactness `C = GM/(Rc²)`
- orbital/dynamical constraints
- independent measurement provenance

## Required controls

Candidates must be tested against ordinary composition/structure models, measurement error, white-dwarf models, neutron-star models, black-hole models and other physically relevant alternatives.

## Decision rule

No object is labelled a neutron-core object from anomaly alone. The experiment produces ranked candidates and explicitly records `SUPPORTED`, `REJECTED` or `UNRESOLVED` states.
